NASA Earthdata Granule Guide

You Downloaded This. Here Is What It Is.

You download a NASA satellite file, open it in GIS software, and the map comes out quietly wrong. We built a Python engine that reads the file and writes a guide to it: what each piece of the filename means, how the numbers were made, and exactly where it will mislead you, with every claim marked as read from the file or inferred.

Self-contained dashboards, each built around one question someone needed answered.

ABA Medicaid Reimbursement Dashboard

Five Mid-Atlantic Medicaid programs give five different answers to who can deliver Applied Behavior Analysis, who can bill for it, and at what rate. We pulled the regulations into one reference: pick a state and see its roles, rates and qualification pathways. A capstone project built from published state policy.

CMMC Level 1 Assessment Dashboard

A small contractor going after defense work has to show it meets CMMC Level 1, and the requirements are buried in long PDFs. We extracted all 58 assessment objectives into one searchable tool, with plain-language summaries, policy templates, and how each objective gets assessed.

Launch dashboard →

DC Property Tax Geospatial Analysis

Does what a DC owner pays line up with what the city says their property is worth? We mapped every parcel's assessment, tax bill and effective rate, so the pattern shows block by block. Switch between five measures to see it from different angles.
